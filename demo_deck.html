The Problem You're Solving

The Perishable Food Paradox:
Tons of Waste, Endless Grind

Commercial kitchens have excess meals ready at 8:00 PM. Perishable food spoils by 11:00 PM. Human coordinators cannot scale to real-time micro-logistics.

3–5 hrs
Daily Manual Busywork
Calling drivers, calculating distance, asking shelters for fridge space, and verifying dietary constraints manually.
180 min
Perishability Deadline
Cooked meals and dairy spoil quickly. A 30-minute delay in manual coordination results in the entire donation being thrown away.
80%
Coordinator Burnout
Non-profit organizers spend their entire day playing frantic dispatchers instead of focusing on human community care.
The Autonomous Solution

Autonomous Micro-Logistics:
Silent Execution, Rare Escalation

Surplus Router acts as an autonomous background engine. Instead of an app humans have to babysit, it coordinates end-to-end automatically.

⚡
Autonomous Pipeline (95% of Cases)
1. Donor reports 25 kg cooked meals via 30-sec form.
2. Strands Agent assesses perishability & queries regional shelter capacity.
3. Multi-factor algorithm finds the optimal shelter and locks capacity in DynamoDB.
4. Agent dispatches nearest volunteer driver with route directions.
No Human Required Sub-Second Coordination
🛡️
Human-in-the-Loop (Only for Real Exceptions)
The agent only interrupts the human coordinator when automated safety cannot be guaranteed:
• Remaining shelf-life under 60 minutes.
• Entire city shelter capacity exhausted.
• Unresolvable claim conflicts.
Zero Coordinator Fatigue Safe Boundary Guardrails
Technical Implementation

Built on Strands SDK & AWS AgentCore

Enterprise-grade architecture leveraging AWS managed serverless primitives for high reliability and zero-leak privacy.

🤖
Strands SDK
Stateful orchestrator coordinating classification, capacity check, and dispatch tools.
⚡
AWS DynamoDB
ACID TransactWriteItems prevents double-allocations across 5 single-digit ms tables.
🔐
Zero-IDOR Security
256-bit cryptographic capability tokens and constant-time SHA-256 verification.
📍
Location Service
Matrix distance routing calculates exact transit times to prioritize nearby shelters.
Live System Proof

Deterministic Multi-Factor
Scoring Algorithm

Total Score = 0.35 × Distance + 0.25 × Capacity + 0.20 × Dietary + 0.20 × Urgency
Guarantees auditable, deterministic outcomes with zero hallucination.
📏
Distance (35%)
Proximity decay function ensures hot cooked meals arrive fresh and transit emissions are minimized.
⚖️
Capacity (25%)
Evaluates shelter daily kg limit. Automatically bypasses near-full shelters to prevent food dumping.
🥗
Dietary & Urgency (40%)
Enforces Halal, vegetarian, and allergen constraints while scaling score with shelf-life urgency.
Safety & Human Oversight

The Coordinator Command Center

Real-time transparency without information overload. Full auditability across all autonomous decisions.

🚨 The Escalation Queue
When an anomaly occurs (e.g. food expires in 45 mins), the agent creates an actionable escalation ticket with full context. The coordinator can dismiss, override, or reassign with 1 click.
📜 Immutable DynamoDB Audit Trail
Every state transition (REPORTED → MATCHED → ASSIGNED → DELIVERED) is committed to frca-matches-audit-dev with actor identity, timestamp, and idempotency key.
